## 📌 Overview  
The **Beauty Salon Management System** is a desktop-based application developed in **C#** with **SQL Server** as the backend.  
It helps salon owners, staff, and customers manage day-to-day operations such as:  
- Booking appointments  
- Managing services, products, and categories  
- Handling payments (cash, card, mobile)  
- Generating bills and reports  
- Collecting customer reviews and ratings  

The system provides a **user-friendly interface** that ensures smooth communication between customers, staff, and admins.  



## 🎯 Objectives  
- Allow customers to **book appointments online**.  
- Enable staff to **manage services, products, and payments**.  
- Provide admins with **easy management tools** for staff, categories, branches, products, and bills.  
- Securely store all user data and ensure role-based access.  
- Generate **accurate billing and reporting** for better business insights.  



## 👥 User Roles  

### 🛠 Admin  
- Manage staff (add/update/delete)  
- Manage products & categories  
- Manage services and branches  
- Generate bills and view balance sheet  
- Monitor reviews and ratings  

### 👩‍💼 Staff  
- Manage personal profile  
- Handle products, categories, and services  
- Manage billing and payments  
- Book appointments for customers  

### 👩 Customer  
- Book appointments  
- Choose services and categories  
- Make payments (cash, card, or mobile wallets)  
- Submit feedback and ratings  



## ✨ Features  

✅ **User Authentication** – Login, Signup, and Password Reset  
✅ **Appointment Booking** – Customers can book services at preferred times  
✅ **Service & Category Management** – Add, edit, and delete services/categories  
✅ **Product Management** – Track product stock, price, and expiry  
✅ **Billing & Payment System** – Cash, card, and mobile payment (bKash, Nagad)  
✅ **Branch Management** – Multi-location support with activation/deactivation  
✅ **Dashboard** – Admin and staff dashboards with reports & stats  
✅ **Feedback & Rating** – Customers provide reviews (1–5 stars)  




## 🛠 Tools & Technologies  
- **Programming Language:** C#  
- **Database:** SQL Server Management Studio  
- **IDE:** Visual Studio 2019
- **Design Approach:** Object-Oriented Programming (OOP)  
  - Encapsulation, Inheritance, Abstraction, Polymorphism  



## 🏗 System Workflow  
1. Users (Admin, Staff, Customer) log in via the same portal.  
2. Customers book appointments by selecting service, category, date, and time.  
3. Staff and Admin access dashboards to manage operations.  
4. Billing is auto-calculated, and customers pay via multiple methods.  
5. Admin can manage multiple salon branches and track reviews/ratings.  



## 🗂️ Database Design  

- **Entities:** User, Product, Service, Category, Appointment, Billing, Branch, Feedback  
- **Normalization:** Up to 3NF (No transitive dependencies)  
- **ER Diagram, Sequence Diagram, Activity Diagram & SQL Queries:** Included in report
